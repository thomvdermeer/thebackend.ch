The invitation

A day to come back
to yourself.

Being Human is a one-day workshop for personal transformation. The day starts from who we are: human beings, with a mind that thinks, emotions that move us, a body that communicates, and an environment we belong to.

Together we explore how to connect with your essence, so that you feel grounded, steady and free to act — also when life asks a lot of you. You learn to recognise what is happening in the moment and how to find your way through it.

The intention

Small practices, done with attention, can quietly change how we live, feel and relate. This day is an invitation to find the ones that are yours.

Three doorways

A full day with three practitioners.

Each of them opens a different doorway.

What you take home

Practical tools to regulate yourself, meet difficulties with more ease, and experience everyday life more profoundly — all simple enough to weave into your routine.

You will leave feeling calm, connected and clear about what matters most to you.

    Do I need any experience? +

    None. Every practice is introduced from the ground up and can be adapted to your body and pace.

    What should I bring? +

    Warm, weather-appropriate clothing and shoes — part of the day is outside, whatever the weather. A water bottle, a light snack, and a notebook. Lunch is provided.

    Is lunch included? +

    Yes — lunch is provided. Bring a light snack for the breaks if you like.
